# ✅ Use Case: Approval Workflow

## 📌 Business Requirement
Ensure controlled spending with proper approvals.

---

## 🌍 Scenario
PR requires approval based on amount, department, or category.

---

## 🔁 Process Flow
1. PR created  
2. Approval triggered  
3. Approver reviews  
4. Approval/Rejection  

---

## ⚙️ System Behavior
- Rule-based approval  
- Multi-level approvals possible  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges
- Delays  
- Incorrect approver mapping  

---

## ✅ Solution
- Define clear approval rules  
- Optimize workflow  

---

## 🎯 Learning
Approval design directly impacts process efficiency.
