# 🛒 Use Case: Catalog vs Non-Catalog Procurement

## 📌 Business Requirement
Organization wants flexibility in purchasing.

---

## 🌍 Scenario
User selects items from catalog OR manually creates request.

---

## 🔁 Process Flow

### Catalog Buying:
- Pre-approved supplier catalog  
- Fixed pricing  
- Faster process  

### Non-Catalog Buying:
- Manual PR creation  
- Requires approval  
- Flexible but slower  

---

## ⚙️ System Behavior
- Catalog → Direct selection  
- Non-catalog → Manual entry  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges
- Price inconsistency  
- Approval delays  

---

## ✅ Solution
- Promote catalog usage  
- Maintain updated catalogs  

---

## 🎯 Learning
Catalog buying improves efficiency and control.
